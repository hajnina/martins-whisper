## Branch review gates

A branch is accepted only if:
1. It has `HANDOFF.md` + `SUMMARY.txt`.
2. Claims are backed by pasted command output or clearly marked blocked.
3. Tests exist for new pure logic and were run (or blocker is explicit).
4. No edits outside its ownership without a note.
5. `git status` clean and commits are atomic.

## Conflict watch

- `app/src/main/AndroidManifest.xml`: U1 vs I1 (permissions/services). Integration resolves.
- `core/.../Contracts.kt`: C1 replaces `TranscriptMerger` stub; I1 replaces `TextInsertionPlanner` stub. Both must keep signatures.
- `app/build.gradle.kts`: U1/E1/B1 may add deps/CMake. Integration merges union, then trims.
- `settings.gradle.kts`/root build: B1 may adjust versions; keep one source of truth.
