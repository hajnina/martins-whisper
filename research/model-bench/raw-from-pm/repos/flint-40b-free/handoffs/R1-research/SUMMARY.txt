R1 recommends ADR-004 Option A: vendor MIT whisper.cpp, build one arm64-v8a library with Vulkan + CPU, default auto with CPU fallback, and surface the backend in doctor/settings.
Keep SPEC overlay shape: TYPE_APPLICATION_OVERLAY + microphone FGS + separate AccessibilityService; TYPE_ACCESSIBILITY_OVERLAY is only a no-SAW fallback.
Insert direct-first (ACTION_SET_TEXT + read-back), then custom paste/ACTION_PASTE, then clipboard + notification; never write password fields; do not auto-clear clipboard.
Implement 300 s idle unload with a single ASR dispatcher, leases, off-main free, and trim handling; accessibility-bound processes may never get onTrimMemory(BACKGROUND).
